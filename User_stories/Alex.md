## Story 1: Important Email Reminders
As a student who often overlooks important emails, I want important university emails to pop up when I open the app, so that I do not miss important announcements, deadlines or meetings.
## Acceptance criteria:
- Given I have opened the app, when I have unread important emails, then they appear as a pop-up notification on the main screen.
- Given I have received an important email, when I have not read it, then the app reminds me until I acknowledge it.
- Given an important email contains a deadline or meeting, when I click the "Add to Calendar" button, then it is added to my calendar with a single click.
## Sprint goal:
The student will be able to view important emails immediately when opening the app, receive reminders and add important dates to their calendar with a single click.
## Story 2: Clubs and Society Events
As a student who wants to socialise and meet new people, I want to view all university club and society events happening each day in one place, so that I do not miss opportunities to participate, socialise and make new friends.
## Acceptance criteria:
- Given I have opened the app, when I access the events section, then I can see all club and society events happening that day.
- Given I am interested in an event, when I select it, then I can view its time, location and description.
- Given I want to attend an event, when I click the "Add to Calendar" button, then the event is added to my calendar with a single click.
- Given I have added an event to my calendar, when the event is approaching, then I receive a reminder.
## Sprint goal:
The student will be able to discover daily club and society events, receive event reminders and participate in more social activities to improve their university experience.
